**We do not sell your personal data**, and we do not share it for third-party advertising.

We share personal data only as needed with:

- **Authorities**, courts or regulators, where the law requires it or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.
- A successor, if the business or its assets are transferred, subject to this notice.

- **access** your personal data and receive a copy;

- **restrict** processing;
- **data portability** for data you provided, processed by automated means on the basis of contract or consent;
- **object** to processing based on legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time;
- **withdraw consent** at any time, where we rely on consent, without affecting earlier processing;
- not be subject to a solely automated decision with legal or similarly significant effects, where Art. 22 applies (see section 3).

To exercise a right, email {{LEGAL_CONTACT_EMAIL}}. We may need to verify your identity. We respond within one month, extendable where the law allows.
