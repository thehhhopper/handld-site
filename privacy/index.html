4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for third-party advertising.

We share personal data only as needed with:

  • Processors acting on our instructions under a data processing agreement: to be confirmed.
  • Hyperliquid, the venue where your account sits and where the desk places orders. Orders and the builder fee placed by the desk are associated with your Hyperliquid address on Hyperliquid. Hyperliquid processes data under its own terms and privacy practices, and activity on Hyperliquid may be publicly visible on its ledger.
  • Stripe, once paid seats exist, for payments. Stripe may act as an independent controller for some purposes under its own privacy policy.
  • Professional advisers (lawyers, accountants, auditors) under confidentiality.
  • Authorities, courts or regulators, where the law requires it or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.
  • A successor, if the business or its assets are transferred, subject to this notice.

7. Security

We use technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including access controls, encryption in transit, and limiting operator access to the audit log. The desk holds only a trade-only agent key that cannot withdraw funds. No system is free of risk. If a personal data breach is likely to put you at high risk, we will tell you without undue delay.

8. Your rights

Where GDPR applies, you have the right to:

  • access your personal data and receive a copy;
  • rectify inaccurate data;
  • erase data, where the law allows. Some data, such as sanctions screening records and audit-log entries, may need to be kept for legal obligations or legal claims;
  • restrict processing;
  • data portability for data you provided, processed by automated means on the basis of contract or consent;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent, without affecting earlier processing;
  • not be subject to a solely automated decision with legal or similarly significant effects, where Art. 22 applies (see section 3).

To exercise a right, email therestishandled@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity. We respond within one month, extendable where the law allows.

If you live outside the EEA, you may have similar rights under your local law.
